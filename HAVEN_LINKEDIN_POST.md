Built HAVEN — an AI-powered safety platform for women.

HAVEN brings together:
- discreet SOS alerts
- trusted contact coordination
- live safety response flows
- AI therapy support
- legal guidance and rights awareness

This project combines FastAPI, Gemini AI, SMTP-based alerting, a React dashboard, and a Flutter mobile experience into one safety-first product.

The goal is simple: help women act faster, feel less alone, and get practical support in moments that matter most.

A strong safety product should be more than a notification. It should feel calm, discreet, and genuinely supportive.

This is what we are building with HAVEN.
